Svelte 5 key points

  • state rune replaces reactive $ label and stores
  • effect rune replaces lifecycle methods likeonMount
  • props rune replaces stores, export syntax, and other complexities.
  • Snippets and slots - In Svelte 4, content can be passed to components using slots. Snippets are more powerful and flexible, and as such slots are deprecated in Svelte 5. - https://svelte-5-preview.vercel.app/docs/snippets#snippets-and-slots
